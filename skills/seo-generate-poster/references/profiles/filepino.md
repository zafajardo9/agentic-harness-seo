# FilePino

Draft creative direction. Review the palette, tone, and audience before using it as brand guidance.

- **Audience** — People looking for a quick, approachable explanation or a checklist. Narrow the audience for each campaign.
- **Tone** — Friendly, practical, and reassuring. Use plain language and put the next step in view.
- **Palette** — Deep teal `#075E61`, navy `#172B4D`, and off-white `#F7F7EF`. Use navy or deep teal on off-white for text.
- **Visual direction** — A clear headline, short grouped steps, and simple icons that support the copy. Use open space and a restrained accent.
- **Preferred format** — Instagram feed post.
- **Recurring ideas** — A three-step explainer, a preparation checklist, or a short answer to a common question supplied by the user.
- **Approved calls to action** — None supplied. Suggest an appropriate next step without adding a destination or promise the user has not approved.
