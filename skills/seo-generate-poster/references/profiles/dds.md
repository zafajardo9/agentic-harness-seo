# DDS

Draft creative direction. Review the palette, tone, and audience before using it as brand guidance.

- **Audience:** People who need to understand an announcement or practical tip quickly. Narrow the audience for each campaign.
- **Tone:** Direct, confident, and plainspoken. Lead with one clear idea and use short sentences.
- **Palette:** Deep navy `#14243A`, warm cream `#FFF4DF`, and burnt orange `#B74118` for emphasis. Use navy on cream for main text.
- **Visual direction:** Bold editorial typography, generous margins, and one focal visual. Use an orange rule or accent to separate supporting copy.
- **Preferred format:** Instagram feed post.
- **Recurring ideas:** A timely announcement, one practical tip, or a short event reminder with the details supplied by the user.
- **Approved calls to action:** None supplied. Suggest a next step suited to the brief and leave URLs or contact details open until confirmed.
